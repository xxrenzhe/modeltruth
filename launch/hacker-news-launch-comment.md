# Hacker News Launch Comment

We built ModelTruth after seeing teams struggle to verify whether OpenAI-compatible endpoints are actually usable for production.

The product deliberately avoids legal labels. It records repeatable technical evidence: availability, TTFT, model behavior consistency, usage metadata and billing variance. Public pages only show redacted aggregate signals and anonymous evidence summaries.

Providers can submit corrections or disputes from the public dispute policy, and reviewed responses are reflected on Provider Truth Boards.

The free path is a stateless Playground and local CLI. The paid path is continuous monitoring for private endpoints with encrypted keys, alerts and exportable evidence.
